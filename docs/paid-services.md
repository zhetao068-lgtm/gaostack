## English

**Setup and handover** is the starting point for editors who want a private workspace across phone and desktop. A scoped engagement can include deployment, access setup, a sample draft acceptance check, and a handover guide. Live publishing depends on the account owner's API permissions.

Draft migration and custom development are also available for inquiry. For migration, start with a sample to check content, summaries, and covers. For custom work, agree on the problem and acceptance criteria first. Deliverables, price, and timing are agreed before work begins.
